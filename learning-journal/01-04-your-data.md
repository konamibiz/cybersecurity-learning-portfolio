Lab: **Analysing a Hotel Customer Data Breach**

Course: Cisco Networking Academy —  Junior Cybersecurity Analyst

Module: 1.1 — Your Data Scenario: Employee credentials used to access a hotel customer database Status: Scenario analysis completed; practical investigation not performed

**What I Learned**

This scenario demonstrates how compromised employee credentials can expose large volumes of customer data. An attacker may use legitimate login details to bypass controls that rely on passwords alone.
It also highlights the importance of protecting personal data, limiting employee permissions and monitoring access to databases.
Important Cybersecurity Concepts
* Data breach and unauthorised access
* Credential compromise
* Data confidentiality and privacy
* Least privilege and access control
* Multi-factor authentication
* Security monitoring and incident response

**Practical Exercise**
I analysed the reported incident to identify the initial access method, the potential impact on customer information and the controls that could reduce the likelihood or impact of a similar incident.
**Findings:**
* Initial access involved compromised employee credentials.
* More than three million guests were reportedly affected.
* The reported investigation had not identified access to account passwords or financial information.
* Further investigation would be needed to establish the full scope of the breach.


**What I Would Do Differently in a Real IT Environment**
I would secure the affected account, revoke active sessions, review authentication and database logs, determine the scope of the exposure and follow the organisation's incident-response procedure.
For prevention, I would recommend MFA, least-privilege database permissions, regular access reviews, centralised security logging and staff awareness training.

**Lessons Learned**
A valid employee login does not necessarily indicate legitimate activity. Organisations need layered security controls that protect data even when an account is compromised.
Evidence limitation: This was an analysis of a supplied scenario. I did not access a real hotel system, investigate production logs or perform live incident-response actions.
