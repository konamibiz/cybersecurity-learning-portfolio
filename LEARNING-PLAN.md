# Learning Plan

## Before starting
- [ ] Open the Cisco Networking Academy cybersecurity page.
- [ ] Enrol in the introductory course or the appropriate beginner pathway.
- [ ] Record the exact course name and the date started.
- [ ] Create a learning-journal entry after the first session.

## Weekly routine
1. Study a course module.
2. Write a short explanation of the concepts in my own words.
3. Complete the available quiz or lab.
4. Record what I tried, what happened, and what I learned.
5. Update the progress tracker and commit the changes to GitHub.

## Rules for documenting learning
- Do not claim a skill until I have practised or can explain it.
- Separate course completion from professional certification.
- Clearly label simulated scenarios.
- Do not upload passwords, tokens, real user data, private IP inventories, confidential screenshots, or employer records.
- Link to course material where appropriate; do not copy large sections of course content.
